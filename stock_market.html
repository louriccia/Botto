Each day runs 4 ticks — 12am the daily challenge pod is announced (its maker gets exposure), 6am that maker's volatility spikes, 12pm a news headline drops, 6pm the news moves that stock's trend. The market trades 6am–5pm on weekdays: while OPEN the broker fee is 2% and sentiment counts 1×; while CLOSED (nights, 6pm, and weekends) the fee is 5% and sentiment counts 2×. Anti-whale rake: big orders pay order-size slippage (a large order moves its own fill price), and once your net worth crosses the whale line 🐋 your broker fees double (4% open / 10% closed) — whales still feel the open-vs-closed swing, just at twice the bite.
